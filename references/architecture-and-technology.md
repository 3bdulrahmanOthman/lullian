# Architecture and Technology Contract

Architecture link: requirements → quality attributes → constraints → alternatives when material → decision → consequences → evidence.

Do not force three architecture options when the choice is obvious/low-risk. Present alternatives when they materially change risk, reversibility, operational burden, or fit.

Technology decisions record candidates, selected option, fit, burden, cost, lock-in, ecosystem/tool support, migration implications, evidence, assumptions, and status.

Conditional architecture is valid while a spike is unresolved.
